Model   Sales adoption is modelled using a logistic (S-curve) function f(t) = L / (1 + e−k(t−t₀)), where L is the market saturation ceiling, k is the growth rate, and t₀ is the inflection year. Parameters are fitted via nonlinear least-squares regression on IEA historical EV sales data. Forecast periods are shown as dashed lines.
