About this chart

  • Tracks total EV sales volume across global markets from early adoption through mass-market acceleration to eventual saturation — following a classic S-curve diffusion pattern.
  • Solid lines show historical actual EV sales drawn directly from IEA source data.
  • Dashed lines show modelled forecast projections of EV sales through 2035, fitted using a logistic curve.
  • The S-curve moves through three phases: early growth (slow initial uptake) → acceleration (rapid mass-market expansion near the inflection year) → saturation (growth flattens as the market approaches its ceiling).
  • Use the Region dropdown to switch between markets. Hover the chart for exact values at each data point.

About this chart

  • Historical actuals — solid lines show real EV sales volume recorded by the IEA, capturing the market as it actually developed from early adoption onward.
  • Forecast projections — dashed lines extend the fitted model through 2035, representing modelled projections rather than confirmed data.
  • S-curve adoption pattern — the trajectory follows a logistic diffusion curve: an initial slow-growth phase as the market establishes itself, a rapid acceleration phase once adoption reaches critical mass, and a flattening saturation phase as the addressable market fills.
  • Region selector — each region has its own fitted curve with independently estimated growth rate (k), inflection year (t₀), and saturation ceiling (L). Use the dropdown above to compare trajectories across markets.
  • Stat tiles — the four tiles below the chart update automatically to show the selected region's projected peak share, inflection year, current share, and recent average growth rate.
Model   Sales adoption is modelled using a logistic (S-curve) function f(t) = L / (1 + e−k(t−t₀)), where L is the market saturation ceiling, k is the growth rate, and t₀ is the inflection year. Parameters are fitted via nonlinear least-squares regression on IEA historical EV sales data. Forecast periods are shown as dashed lines.
