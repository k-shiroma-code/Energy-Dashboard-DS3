Methodology   EV sales forecasts are derived from IEA historical data, projected via logistic S-curve regression. Oil displacement assumes each EV offsets ~1,300 gallons/year (≈ 31 barrels). GDP savings are computed using country-specific import cost-per-barrel and nominal GDP. Adoption rate multiplier scales projected sales linearly for scenario analysis.
